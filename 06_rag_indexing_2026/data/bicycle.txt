Bicycle maintenance basics.

A puncture is the most common roadside problem. To repair it, remove the wheel, lever the tyre off the rim and pull out the inner tube. Pump a little air into the tube and listen for the hiss, or dip it in water and watch for bubbles. Roughen the area with sandpaper, apply a patch and wait until the glue is dry before putting everything back.

The chain needs attention every few hundred kilometres. Wipe off old grime with a rag, apply one drop of lubricant to each link and spin the pedals backwards. Wipe away the excess, because a wet chain collects sand and wears out faster.

Brakes should bite firmly before the lever touches the handlebar. If they feel soft, tighten the cable with the barrel adjuster or replace worn pads.
