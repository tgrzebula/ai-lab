Brewing coffee at home.

Espresso is made by forcing hot water at around nine bars of pressure through finely ground coffee. A shot takes 25 to 30 seconds. If it runs faster, grind finer; if it drips slowly, grind coarser.

Beans from the island of Java in Indonesia have a heavy body and earthy taste. Many blends mix Java beans with lighter Ethiopian coffee to balance them.

Store beans in an airtight container away from light. Grind just before brewing, because ground coffee loses its aroma within minutes.
